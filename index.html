Closet Photographs

Snap pictures of laid-out clothes, one person's things per batch. Pick whose before you add — or tap the name on any photo after — and every item cataloged from it is theirs.

Photos sync between your devices as compressed copies. Pressing “Analyze” sends that photo to Claude; nothing else leaves your accounts.

The Collection

Everything Claude found, plus anything you add by hand. Tap the dot to change whose it is; tap the name to see its photo and set where it lives.

The Consultation

Every good stylist starts with questions. Answer for each of you — what you wear most earns the easiest-to-reach spots. Answers sync to both phones, and "Place Every Item" has Claude give every cataloged piece a consistent home based on them.

The Edit — Keep, Donate, or Toss

A great closet starts with less. Photograph anything on the fence — solo, or a few pieces laid flat on the bed — and get an honest verdict on its condition. Fit can't be judged from a photo, so "does this still fit" gets you sent to the mirror with instructions.

Claude API key

The Wardrobe and Outfits features call Claude directly from your browser. Paste an Anthropic API key (from platform.claude.com) — it's stored only in this browser, never uploaded anywhere else.

Sync between devices

Optional: keep your wardrobe catalog and saved designs in sync across all your devices. Paste a GitHub personal access token (classic) with only the gist scope — from github.com → Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens. Use the same token on every device. Your wardrobe catalog, saved designs, and compressed copies of your closet photos all sync.
